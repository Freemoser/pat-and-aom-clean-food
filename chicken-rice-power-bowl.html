Chicken Rice Power Bowl (ข้าวอกไก่คลีน)

The Chicken Rice Power Bowl is our clean fuel version of a classic performance meal: lean protein, quality carbs and balanced nutrition in one bowl.

It’s one of the best options in Chiang Mai if you want a healthy high-protein meal that supports fitness goals without sacrificing taste.

Macros & Nutrition

  • Calories: ~540 kcal
  • Protein: ~45g
  • Carbs: ~50g
  • Fat: ~12g

Macronutrient Deep Dive

This bowl is built around performance nutrition.

Chicken breast delivers a strong protein base for muscle maintenance and recovery. Rice gives your body efficient carbohydrates for training intensity, daily energy and recovery. Vegetables add fiber, vitamins and texture while keeping the meal fresh and light.

Because the fats stay relatively low, the bowl digests well and works great as both a lunch and post-workout meal.

The Story Behind Chicken Rice Bowls

Rice bowls have always been part of Asian food culture because they’re simple, affordable and nutritionally balanced.

Modern fitness culture adapted this concept into “macro bowls” and meal prep bowls. At P&O Clean Food Chiang Mai, we combine both worlds: Asian comfort food structure with modern sports nutrition principles.

Healthy food should still feel satisfying — not like punishment.
