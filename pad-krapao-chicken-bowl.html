Pad Krapao Chicken Bowl (ผัดกะเพราไก่) — High Protein Thai Comfort Food in Chiang Mai

If you’re looking for a clean version of one of Thailand’s most iconic street food dishes in Chiang Mai, our Pad Krapao Chicken Bowl is built for exactly that. We keep the bold holy basil flavor, garlic, chili and umami profile — but remove the heavy oil and unnecessary calories you normally get from traditional street food versions.

Macros & Nutrition

  • Calories: ~520 kcal
  • Protein: ~42g
  • Carbs: ~48g
  • Fat: ~14g

Macronutrient Deep Dive

This meal is designed for gym performance, recovery and satiety.

The lean chicken gives you a high amount of complete protein, helping muscle recovery and keeping you full longer. Jasmine rice provides fast and efficient carbohydrates for training energy and glycogen replenishment. The fats stay moderate, so the bowl feels satisfying without becoming heavy or greasy.

Holy basil, garlic and chili also naturally support digestion and metabolism — which is one reason this dish became so popular across Thailand in the first place.

The Story Behind Pad Krapao

Pad Krapao (ผัดกะเพรา) is one of the most loved everyday meals in Thailand. Originally it became popular because it was fast, spicy, cheap and easy to cook for workers and students.

The key ingredient is holy basil (“krapao”), which gives the dish its signature aroma and slightly peppery taste. In Thailand, it’s comfort food. Almost every local has their favorite version.

At P&O Clean Food Chiang Mai, we wanted to keep the authentic flavor while making it fit a healthier lifestyle and gym-focused nutrition plan.
