Protein Chicken Wrap (แรปไก่โปรตีนสูง)

Our Protein Chicken Wrap is one of the most popular healthy grab-and-go meals in Chiang Mai for people who want something quick, high protein and actually filling.

Instead of greasy fast food wraps, we use lean chicken breast, fresh vegetables and a balanced sauce inside a soft tortilla wrap that works perfectly before or after training.

Macros & Nutrition

  • Calories: ~490 kcal
  • Protein: ~38g
  • Carbs: ~36g
  • Fat: ~16g

Macronutrient Deep Dive

The wrap is balanced to give sustained energy without a food coma.

Lean chicken provides high-quality protein with low saturated fat. Fresh vegetables add fiber and micronutrients while helping digestion and fullness. The tortilla supplies moderate carbs for stable energy, making this a great meal before Muay Thai, gym training or even a healthy lunch while exploring Chiang Mai.

Compared to typical fast food wraps, this version is significantly cleaner and more macro-friendly.

The Story Behind the Wrap

Wrap culture originally became popular in Western fitness and meal prep communities because it’s practical, portable and easy to customize.

In Chiang Mai, healthy convenience food is growing fast, especially among digital nomads, gym members and health-conscious travelers. We created our Protein Wrap to combine Western fitness nutrition with fresh Thai ingredients and local flavor preferences.

Simple, high protein, no nonsense.
