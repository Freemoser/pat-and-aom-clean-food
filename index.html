Our Story

Hi, we’re Pat & Aom.

We started this journey with a simple idea:
We love Thai food — and we love eating clean.

As fitness enthusiasts, we always struggled to find meals that are both truly healthy and full of authentic Thai flavor. So we decided to create it ourselves.

Our mission is to bring Thai cuisine to a new level —
clean, balanced, and made for your lifestyle.

That means:
No unnecessary oil.
No added sugar.
Just real ingredients, traditional recipes, and food that fuels your body.

We’re building more than just one place —
we’re building a concept. A growing network of clean Thai kitchens that make healthy eating easy, accessible, and exciting.

You can follow our journey on social media as we grow, experiment, and keep improving every single dish.

Right now, you can already order and be part of our story.
And soon, we’ll be ready to welcome you in our restaurant.

One thing you shouldn’t miss:
our Meal Prep concept — designed for active people who want to stay consistent without sacrificing taste.

At the end of the day, everything we do is simple:
We want to make it easy for you to eat fresh, healthy, and truly delicious Thai food — anytime.
